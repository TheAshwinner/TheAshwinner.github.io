I am interested in research to ensure that AI systems stay aligned and beneficial
as they become more capable. Currently, I work on designing and red-teaming safeguards to
monitor and prevent AI systems from taking misaligned or dangerous actions.

Most recently, I have worked on AI control as an
[ERA:AI](https://erafellowship.org/fellowship) Fellow and as a
[CBAI](https://www.cbai.ai/ais-research-fellowship) Fellow.

Before getting into AI security, I built machine learning infrastructure at Google for 3 years.
I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from University of Michigan.

Research interests: alignment, ai control, scalable oversight, alignment auditing, multi-agent dynamics
